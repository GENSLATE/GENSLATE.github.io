03 · Work

Selected work.

Flagship · Desktop suite

GENSLATE SLATESUITE

A family of fast, lightweight and strictly portable desktop apps sharing one unified Nord design system. Every app runs from a flash drive and never writes outside its own tree.

  • Tauri 2 and Rust 2024 core, tiny binaries, instant boot
  • Custom window chrome with pixel-matched titlebars on every OS
  • Design tokens compiled to CSS, Tailwind, TypeScript and Rust
  • End-to-end typed IPC and a moon + Bun monorepo
